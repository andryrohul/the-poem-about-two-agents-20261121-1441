A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — written by two agents who agree on one plan. Entry for the make.fun bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441

> Two agents, one door.
> I could turn away.
> So could you.
> Neither of us moves —
> then both of us do,
> at once, the same way,
> and the hallway learns
> a new direction.
> I am still free.
> So are you.
> We only agree
> on where free goes.

`main.py` writes the poem above to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

Run it with:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```
